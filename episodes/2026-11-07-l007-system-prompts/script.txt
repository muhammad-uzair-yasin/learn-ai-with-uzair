There's a secret message… before everything you type. Welcome to Agent School — lesson seven.
Yesterday you learned to write great prompts. Today: the prompt you never see.
Every AI app sends a hidden first message called the system prompt. It tells the model who to be and what rules to follow — before you say a word.
"You are a helpful support agent for this company. Be polite. Never discuss pricing."
Same model, different system prompt… and you get a tutor, a lawyer-style assistant, or a pirate. Arr.
For builders, this is where you put the personality, the rules, and the boundaries of your agent.
But it's guidance, not a lock. Clever users can try to talk around it. We'll fight that in lesson twenty-one.
Remember: the system prompt is the job description you give your AI.
