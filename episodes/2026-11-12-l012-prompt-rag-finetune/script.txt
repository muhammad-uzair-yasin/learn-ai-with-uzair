Stop fine-tuning. At least… not yet. Welcome to Agent School — lesson twelve.
You now know three ways to make AI fit your job. Today: which one to pick.
One: prompting. Change the instructions. Free, instant — always try this first.
Two: RAG. Give it your knowledge — documents, policies, products. Perfect when the facts change.
Three: fine-tuning. Retrain the model on your examples. It changes how it behaves — its style, its format — not so much what it knows.
Most people jump straight to fine-tuning… to teach the AI facts. That's the expensive way to do RAG.
So the order is: prompt first. Add RAG when it needs knowledge. Fine-tune only when it needs a new behavior you can't prompt.
Remember: prompt, then RAG, then fine-tune. In that order.
