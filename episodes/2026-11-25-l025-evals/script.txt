Your agent passed your test… and failed a hundred users. Welcome to Agent School — lesson twenty-five.
You tried it three times. It worked. You shipped it. Then real people arrived.
Real users ask in ways you never imagined. Typos, slang, weird edge cases. And AI is random — it can pass once and fail the next time.
That's why builders use evals: a test set of real examples, each with what a good answer looks like. Fifty, a hundred, more.
Every time you change the prompt or the model, run all of them — and get a score. Did it get better… or worse?
And every failure a user reports becomes a new test. Your eval set grows smarter every week.
Remember: one good demo proves nothing. A test set with a score proves progress.
