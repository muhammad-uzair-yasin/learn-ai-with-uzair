AI can't see the word "strawberry." Seriously. Welcome to Agent School — lesson three.
Yesterday: machines learn from examples. Today: what those examples actually look like to the machine.
AI doesn't read letters. It reads tokens — chunks of text. "Strawberry" might become "straw"… and "berry."
Each token becomes a number. So the model sees a short list of numbers — not ten letters.
That's why some models famously miscounted the R's in strawberry. They never saw the letters!
In English, one token is roughly three quarters of a word. A hundred words? About a hundred and thirty tokens.
And tokens matter for you, because AI is priced in tokens, and its memory is measured in tokens.
Remember: AI doesn't read words. It reads tokens.
