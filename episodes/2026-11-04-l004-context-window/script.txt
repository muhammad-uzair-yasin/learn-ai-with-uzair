Your AI forgets you… in the middle of a conversation. Welcome to Agent School — lesson four.
Yesterday: AI reads tokens. Today: how many tokens it can hold at once.
Every model has a context window — its working memory. Your messages, its replies, any files… all must fit inside.
Picture a desk. Everything on the desk, the AI can see. Fall off the desk… and it's gone.
Chat long enough and the window fills up. The oldest messages slide off the edge. That rule you set an hour ago? It might not be on the desk anymore.
Windows are huge today — hundreds of thousands of tokens on many models. But huge isn't infinite, and a crowded desk makes answers worse.
So for long work: restate what matters, or start a fresh chat with a short summary.
Remember: if it's not in the context window, the AI doesn't know it.
