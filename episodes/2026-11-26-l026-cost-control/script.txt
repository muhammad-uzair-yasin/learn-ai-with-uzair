An agent loop can empty your wallet… overnight. Welcome to Agent School — lesson twenty-six.
Lesson twenty-three: every call costs tokens. Now picture a loop that never stops.
A tool keeps failing. The agent keeps retrying. Each retry sends the whole growing conversation again. Thousands of calls… while you sleep.
Four guards. One: a maximum number of steps per task. Two: a budget limit per task and per day. Three: smaller models for simple steps. Four: caching — don't pay twice for the same answer.
And set billing alerts with your provider. If spending jumps, you hear about it before the bill arrives.
Most surprise bills are bugs, not busy users.
Remember: every loop needs a step limit and a budget.
