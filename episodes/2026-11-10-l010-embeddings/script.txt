AI turns every sentence… into coordinates. Welcome to Agent School — lesson ten.
Yesterday: RAG finds the right page in your files. Today: how it finds it.
An embedding turns text into a long list of numbers — a point on a giant map of meaning.
On this map, "puppy" lands near "dog." "Refund policy" lands near "how do I get my money back" — even though they share almost no words.
That's the magic. Search by meaning, not by matching words.
So RAG turns your question into a point, finds the closest points in your documents, and hands those chunks to the AI.
These maps have hundreds or thousands of dimensions. We can't picture them — but the math of distance still works.
Remember: embeddings put meaning on a map. Close points, similar meaning.
