This is the USB-C of AI. Welcome to Agent School — lesson sixteen.
Yesterday: tools let AI act. Today's problem: every app needed its own custom tool code.
MCP — the Model Context Protocol — fixes that. Anthropic released it as an open standard in late 2024.
Remember drawers full of different chargers? USB-C made one plug fit everything. MCP does that for AI tools.
Build an MCP server once — for your calendar, your database, your files — and any AI app that speaks MCP can plug into it.
And it spread fast. OpenAI, Google and many others added support.
A plug that fits everything also needs care: only connect servers you trust, and give them the least access they need.
Remember: build the tool once. Plug it into any AI. That's MCP.
