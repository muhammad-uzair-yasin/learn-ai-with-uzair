Build your agent from scratch… or use a framework? Welcome to Agent School — lesson twenty-four.
By now you know an agent is a loop, tools, and a model. That's surprisingly little code.
From scratch: a few dozen lines. You see every step, you control everything, and nothing is hidden. Perfect for learning — and for simple agents.
Frameworks give you ready-made pieces: memory, multi-agent teams, tracing, retries. Faster for big systems.
But they also hide things. And when something breaks inside a framework you don't understand… good luck.
So here's the rule. Build your first agent from scratch, to learn the loop. Reach for a framework when you need what it gives you — not before.
Remember: learn the loop by hand. Then let a framework save you time.
