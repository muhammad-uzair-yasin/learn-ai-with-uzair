Give an agent a huge task… and watch it slice it up. Welcome to Agent School — lesson eighteen.
Our agent can think, act, and remember. But big goals break simple loops.
"Plan my product launch." That's not one step. It's fifty.
So good agents plan first. They break the goal into small steps: research competitors, draft the announcement, design the visuals, schedule the posts.
Then they work through the list one by one — and tick things off.
And the best part: they re-plan. A step fails? The agent adjusts the plan instead of giving up.
Planning also makes agents easier to trust. You can read the plan before anything happens — and approve it.
Remember: big task, small steps. Plan, do, check, re-plan.
